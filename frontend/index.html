Formula. Rate change = policy shock × pass-through ÷ 10,000. Affected balance = eligible balance × exposure share × repriced share. Annualized run rate = affected balance × borrowing-rate change. Uniform timing accumulates at half the horizon's full run rate; start timing applies the selected repriced share from month 0. The mechanical comparator assumes full balance, one-for-one pass-through, and immediate repricing.

Balances are held constant. Repayments, defaults, new borrowing, rate floors/caps, taxes, compounding, and borrower/product-level repricing schedules are not modeled. Cost over a finite horizon is an undiscounted sum, not an annual rate or forecast.
